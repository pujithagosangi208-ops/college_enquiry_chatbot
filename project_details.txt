PROJECT TITLE:
AI-Based College Enquiry Chatbot

PROBLEM STATEMENT:
Students need information about college courses,
admissions, fees, scholarships, hostel facilities,
transport and placements. Searching for these details
from different sources can be inconvenient.

PROPOSED SOLUTION:
We are developing a college enquiry chatbot that
allows students to ask questions on a website and
receive relevant answers.

TARGET USERS:
Students, parents and prospective students.

TECHNOLOGIES:
Python, Streamlit and keyword-based question matching.

FUTURE IMPROVEMENT:
Improve question understanding using NLP or an AI model
and maintain verified college information.
